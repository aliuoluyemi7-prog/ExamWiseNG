2. Known subjects (optional)

Comma-separated subject whitelist

Questions with a subject not in this list are flagged as "unknown subject" (a warning, not a hard error — useful for catching typos like "Economic" instead of "Economics").

3. Expected duplicates (optional)

Ignore keywords (checked against the "tags" field)

Near-identical or exact-duplicate questions are downgraded from Warning to Info when either (a) they share the same non-empty passage_id — e.g. a cloze passage series — or (b) both share one of these tag keywords. This keeps intentional repetition (like 8 gap-fill questions on one passage) from cluttering the warnings you actually need to review.
